Meet Lela · Lakewood, Ohio

The Heart Behind the Whisk

Hello, I’m Lela. I’m more than the founder of Lela's Golden Whisk Bakery — I’m its soul.

From my cozy home bakery in Lakewood, Ohio, I pour love, intention, and a whole lot of butter into every single creation. A devoted wife, nurturing mom, and loyal friend, I bring the same care to my baking as I do to every relationship in my life. My kitchen isn’t just where recipes come to life — it’s where memories are made, laughter is shared, and joy is baked into every bite.

A Legacy of Love & Flour

This bakery carries the legacy of fierce, incredible women who baked with heart. My mom was a baker. So was my mother-in-law. Their kitchens were filled with warmth and the kind of magic that only rises in the oven. For years, even while working in retail, I found myself drawn back to the sweet science of baking—sneaking in late-night cookie experiments, dreaming up cake flavors, and imagining a place where joy could be served by the dozen.

Today, that dream is a reality right here in the Lakewood community.

Sweets That Feel Like Hugs

From the first swirl of frosting to the final dusting of edible glitter, each creation reflects my belief that desserts should feel like comfort. Whether I’m whipping up golden cupcakes for a birthday, crafting whimsical cookies for a baby shower, or designing bedazzled cakes that sparkle with personality, I pour my heart into every detail. My signature style — playful, elegant, and joyfully over-the-top — mirrors my own spirit: radiant, generous, and full of life.

Lela's Golden Whisk is a love letter to the people who inspire me daily — my husband, who cheers me on; our children, who taste-test with delight; and my friends, who remind me that sweetness is best when shared.

We blend elegance with playfulness, crafting custom sweets that shimmer with personality. Welcome to our celebration of heritage, heart, and the belief that joy should be shared, one bite at a time.
